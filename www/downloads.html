0.99.7 highlights

  • The composer stays with a submitted turn until it reports activity, then returns after a brief bounded delay if no activity starts.
  • Every request declares the complete native tool catalog, with durable timers and runtime-owned IRC connection controls available when their current state permits them.
  • Malformed Responses stream records keep strict rejection while retaining a content-free protocol-shape diagnostic for support.

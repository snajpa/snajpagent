0.99.6 highlights

  • Images, documents and sampled video are read natively, and Office work runs through the engine installed on the target.
  • Long sessions cost less: a stable per-conversation identity lets the provider reuse its cached prefix — 87.5% of input tokens in the first measurement, against 30.0% before — with /status reporting cached and uncached input.
  • Steadier provider continuity: a tool call keeps the provider's own call id on both sides of a request, so a scope change can no longer mismatch a call and its result.
